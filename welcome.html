3 · Three things, about five minutes

Your program is live the moment you sign up. These make it yours.

🎁 Decide what they earn

So a $18 order on a first visit is 50 + 180 = 230 points — most of the way to a free side. Set these to match your margins.
2

🪧 Print your QR poster

In the app: Marketing Tools → Get my QR poster. Print it, tape it in the window. One scan gets a customer the app and joins them to your rewards — no codes to type, no sign-up sheet.

Point at it and say "scan here for free food." That one line does more than anything else on this page.
3

📍 Post where you're parked — and clear it when you leave

Tap Today's Location on your dashboard and put in the address. Customers can see which trucks are out and where, so people looking for food can find you.

This one matters: the address stays up until you take it down. When you're done for the day, switch ordering off and the app will ask if you want to clear it — one tap. You can also hit Clear next to the address any time.

If you leave yesterday's address up, someone can drive across town to an empty parking space. That's the fastest way to lose a customer, so it's worth the one tap.
