🚀 Getting Started

From setup to your first rewarded customer

💡
Your loyalty program is already live. Do these 3 things once and you're running — most trucks are set up in about 5 minutes.
1
🎁 Set up what customers earn
Pick the rewards they can redeem (like a free drink), your points rate, and any spin-wheel prizes. This is what makes coming back worth it.
2
📲 Get customers on the app
Print your QR poster and stick it on your truck window. When a customer scans it, they get the app and join your rewards in one scan — no codes to type. Point to it and say "scan here for free food."
3
🪟 Reward them at the window
When they order, ask if they're on your rewards. Scan their code (or look them up by phone), enter the order total, and award points. If they've earned a reward, they'll show a code — enter it to confirm.
4
📍 Clear your location when you leave
Today's Location stays on the map until you take it down — it does not clear itself overnight. When you're done, switch ordering off and the app will offer to clear it in one tap, or hit Clear next to the address any time.
💡
Leaving yesterday's address up sends someone to an empty parking space. It's the quickest way to lose a customer.

⚙️ Points Rules

💡
How customer earning works
Check-in QR scan → customer gets visit points + 1 spin on the wheel per day at your truck.

At the window → you award points based on their order total. No spin for this method — just points.

Want points-only, no spin? Just post your QR code at the truck and use the Award Points window — skip the spin prizes entirely.

Points are per-truck — customers earn and spend points separately at each truck they join.
💰 Order-Based Earning
Example: Customer spends $15 → 200 pts (50 base + 150 order)
🔥 Bonus Rules
Double Points — First Visit
New members get 2× on their first ever check-in
Streak Multiplier (1.5×)
Visiting consecutive days earns 1.5× points
Birthday Bonus (100pts)
Auto award on customer's birthday if they've added it
Win-Back (30 days inactive)
Auto-send 50pts to customers who haven't visited in 30 days

📅 Weekly Schedule

Let customers know where you'll be

💡
Customers can see your weekly schedule in the app. Fill in the days you operate — leave blank for days you're closed. Full street addresses + ZIP show up best on Maps (e.g. "123 Main St, Mansfield").
📍
Heads up: posting a Today's Location from your dashboard overrides that day's scheduled stop until you clear it — customers see the live location instead. Clear it when you leave, or switch ordering off and the app will offer to do it for you.
